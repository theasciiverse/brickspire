# Roadmap

Brickspire is at 0.8.0 (2026-10-28): the whole climb plays, five towers
and the ending; music, tuning and the open decisions are still to come.
1.0.0 is when everything below is in.

## 0.8.0: built

- [x] Five themed towers, 50 floors, one new brick type per tower
- [x] The paddle on a line, an arc (mini-bosses) and a circle (roofs)
- [x] Five mini-bosses and five roof bosses, the Mason last
- [x] The story: the stars go out tower by tower; the ending lights them
  again, then a second lap
- [x] Power-ups: Plank, Mortar, Chisel, Hourglass, Flock, Spare, Stair
- [x] Synthesised sound effects, the title, the best score kept
- [x] Keyboard, mouse and gamepad; session logging

## 0.9.0: the top of the climb

- [ ] Tower 5's mini-boss confirmed (the Mason's apprentice, built
  provisionally) or replaced
- [ ] How hard the fights at the top are, tuned from play

## 1.0.0

- [ ] Music
- [ ] Everything above played through and tuned
